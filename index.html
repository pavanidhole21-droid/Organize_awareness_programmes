🔄 The Awareness-to-Action Lifecycle

How JAGRUK bridges the gap between seeing a problem and fixing it sustainably.

CEP Proven Workflow
Step 1

Report a Need

Residents submit non-emergency issues with approximate area & optional photo.

Step 2

Moderator Reviews

Validates report, detects duplicates, redacts sensitive info, and approves for action.

Step 3

Organizer Creates Campaign

Converts verified report into an organized activity with goals and capacity limits.

Step 4

Volunteer & Act

Community members register, check-in with digital passes, and execute the drive.

Step 5

Verified Impact

Outcomes, photos & metrics are submitted, moderator-approved, and posted to public ledger.
